UNIT 01
am / is / are
A情境导入 · 认识 Lisa
My name is Lisa.
I'm 22.
I'm not married.
I'm American. I'm from Chicago.
My favourite colour is blue.
I'm a student.
My favourite sports are football and swimming.
My father is a doctor and my mother is a journalist.
I'm interested in art.
— LISA —
B语法规则 · 肯定式与否定式
am / is / are be 动词现在时完整变化表
AFFIRMATIVE · 肯定式
Iam(I'm)
he/she/itis(he's / she's / it's)
we/you/theyare(we're / you're / they're)
NEGATIVE · 否定式
Iam not(I'm not)
he/she/itis not(isn't / he's not…)
we/you/theyare not(aren't / we're not…)
  • I'm cold. Can you close the window, please?
  • I'm 32 years old. My sister is 29.
  • Steve is ill. He's in bed.
  • My brother is afraid of dogs.
  • It's ten o'clock. You're late again.
  • Ann and I are good friends.
  • Your keys are on the table.
  • I'm tired, but I'm not hungry.
  • Tom isn't interested in politics. He's interested in music.
  • Jane isn't a teacher. She's a student.
  • Those people aren't English. They're Australian.
  • It's sunny today, but it isn't warm.
C特殊缩略 · that's / there's / here's
CONTRACTIONS · 缩略形式
that is→that's
there is→there's
here is→here's
  • □ Thank you. That's very kind of you.
  • □ Look! There's Chris.
  • □ 'Here's your key.' 'Thank you.'
